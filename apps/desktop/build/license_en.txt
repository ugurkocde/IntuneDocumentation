Use of the collection and export features requires an active subscription or trial license key. A Pro license covers one Microsoft Entra tenant on up to five installations. An MSP license covers the number of tenants in the subscription.

The app reads your Microsoft Intune configuration with your own Entra app registration and read-only Microsoft Graph permissions. Tenant configuration, access tokens, and generated documents stay on your device. To validate the license, the app sends the license key, a random installation id, the tenant id, the operating system, and the app version to the licensing service.
