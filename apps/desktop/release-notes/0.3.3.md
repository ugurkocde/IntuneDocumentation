## New

### HIPAA Security Rule
Compliance Evidence now includes the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C), for organisations that handle electronic protected health information in the United States.

- Nine safeguards are mapped to Intune evidence, including workstation security, encryption and decryption, audit controls, integrity and person or entity authentication.
- Each safeguard shows whether the rule marks it Required, Addressable or Standard.
- The report covers 9 of the 54 standards and implementation specifications. Administrative and physical safeguards, your risk analysis and your decisions on addressable specifications still need separate review.
- The evidence report, the JSON evidence record and the management report all support the new framework.

The report states the technical evidence found in your tenant. It does not certify HIPAA compliance.
