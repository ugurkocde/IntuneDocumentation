## New

### Evidence register and Purview evidence pack
Compliance Evidence gains an Exports section on every framework screen.

- **Evidence register (Excel and CSV).** The assessment as tables: one row per control and one row per technical check, with policy, setting, expected value, actual value and assignment. The Excel workbook adds an About sheet with the status definitions and a Policy status sheet with Intune's device counts per policy. The CSV file holds the checks.
- **Purview evidence pack (ZIP).** One file to attach in Microsoft Purview Compliance Manager, with the framework evidence PDF, the JSON evidence record, an index of every assessed control with the matching Purview template name and where to find it in the PDF, and the upload steps. Control IDs follow the regulation, so check them against your tenant's template before you attach the pack. The pack is evidence for your assessment, not a verdict on any control.

### Draft fix pack
For safeguards with no evidence at all, the app can save draft Windows Settings Catalog policies as JSON.

- Every draft is unassigned and has no effect until you review it, import it in the Intune admin center and assign it yourself. The app never writes to your tenant.
- Drafts are only generated when a complete collection established that the setting is really missing and every value is verified. Settings that are configured differently, conflicting or already set in an unassigned policy get a reason instead of a draft, so you change or assign the existing policy.
- Saving asks you to read four warnings first. The pack repeats them in its README and in every policy description, including: assign each policy to a pilot group first.

### History: encrypted snapshots on your computer
Turn on Keep history on this computer and the app saves a snapshot after each collection.

- Snapshots are stored encrypted on your computer and never leave it. The encryption protects them if the disk or your user folder is copied and from other users of the computer. It does not protect against software that runs under your own account. If secure storage is not available, history stays off and the app says why. Snapshots are never stored without encryption.
- A snapshot keeps the policy settings, assignments and the assessment made at collection time. Script contents, app payloads and details the comparison does not need are not kept. Up to 30 snapshots per tenant are kept for up to 365 days and up to 500 MB in total, oldest removed first.
- The new History screen compares two snapshots: policies added, removed, renamed and changed, setting values before and after, assignment changes, and control status changes with the evidence that caused them. Parts of a partial collection are marked as not compared, never shown as removed.
- Clear local data also removes the snapshot history, its index and its key.

### Scheduled collection
With history on, the app can collect automatically every 6, 12 or 24 hours. It runs only while the app is open and you are signed in, checks the license before every run, saves a snapshot like a manual collection, and runs once after waking if a run was due while the computer slept. It never interrupts work already running, and the History screen shows the next run and the result or reason of the last one.

### Compare with a reference or another tenant
The new Compare screen checks this tenant's settings against a standard, setting by setting.

- Export a reference file from any tenant and load it later or on another computer. The file holds configured settings only, without assignments and without secret values. Its checksum detects damage to the file, not who made it.
- Results show what is different, missing, extra, not comparable and matching, with the values and policies on both sides.
- On the MSP plan, you can compare directly with the latest snapshot of another tenant that has history on this computer.

### Portfolio (MSP plan)
The new Portfolio screen lists every tenant with history on this computer side by side: snapshot count, how old the latest snapshot is, the ruleset that assessed it, and evidence coverage for the framework you choose. Give each tenant a name, and delete a tenant's history when you stop documenting it. The figures come from the encrypted index, so no snapshot is opened to show them.

### DORA framework
Compliance Evidence now includes DORA, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 with its regulatory technical standards, as a desktop only framework. The mapped points provide supporting evidence only. Governance, incident reporting, resilience testing and third party risk need their own assessment, and the report says so per control.

### More evidence sources, no new permissions
- The tenant authentication methods policy and security defaults in Entra are now read with the Policy.Read.All permission the app already requests for Conditional Access. They provide supporting evidence for controls about authentication. The app never suggests turning on security defaults while Conditional Access policies exist, because the two cannot be combined. No new permissions are needed.
- A configured screen lock after inactivity, on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, now counts as supporting evidence where a framework asks for it.

## Improved
- **Documentation export in the new report design.** The PDF and Word documentation now follow the look of the compliance reports: a cover with the tenant, collection date and classification, an At a glance page with assigned, not assigned and unknown counts, a linked table of contents, clearer section headings and tables, and headers and footers with page numbers. Your branding colours, fonts, logos, header and footer text, watermark and confidentiality notice still apply. The coloured coverage grade is gone; the counts speak for themselves.
- **Device status counts where you browse.** Policy lists show Intune's device counts per policy (succeeded, error, conflict, not applicable, pending), and the evidence report gains the same counts as an annex. They describe rollout only and never change a control status. A policy with no reported status says so instead of showing zero.
- **Changes since the previous collection.** With history on, the management summary and the exported management report (English and German) show what changed since the previous snapshot: policy, setting and assignment change counts, and how many measures gained or lost evidence. Measures that could not be fully assessed in one of the two collections, for example because a policy could not be read, are listed apart and never counted as gained or lost.
- Checks that depend on an Entra tenant policy that could not be read now show as unable to check, with the reason, instead of reading as missing evidence.
- Update rings without a quality update deadline and Defender scans set to user defined now read as not configured, with a plain reason, instead of unable to check. Where Windows or Defender use a documented default, such as a 2 day grace period or a 2:00 AM scan, the check uses it and says so.
- Values Microsoft Graph did not return, or returned in a form the app does not know, are shown as unable to check or not compared. They no longer read as a gap, as evidence or as a change in History.

## Fixed
- Word documentation exports now include the header and footer you set in branding. They were left out before.
- A long confidentiality notice or footer text is no longer cut off in the PDF.
- A policy that Intune reports no device status for is no longer indistinguishable from one with zero devices. It is shown as no status reported in the policy lists, the evidence register and the report annex.
