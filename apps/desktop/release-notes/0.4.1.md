## New

### NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC-2:2024)
Compliance Evidence now includes the Essential Cybersecurity Controls of the Saudi National Cybersecurity Authority, ECC-2:2024, as a desktop only framework.

- Ten controls of the Cybersecurity Defense domain receive supporting evidence from your Intune and Entra ID settings: multi-factor sign-in, privileged access, workstation protection, malware protection, patching, mobile device security, encryption of stored data and security event logging. The report lists what each control still needs beyond the settings.
- Supporting evidence only. Governance, resilience, third party and cloud controls, and the process controls of the other subdomains, cannot be checked from the Intune configuration. The report and the management summary list them as outside Intune scope, one row per subdomain, so you can assess them separately.
- Control titles and summaries are our own short wording. Only the Arabic text published by the National Cybersecurity Authority is binding.
- The Purview evidence pack names the Compliance Manager template "Saudi Arabia - National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA)". The template name does not state the ECC version, so check in your tenant that it covers ECC-2:2024 before you attach evidence.
