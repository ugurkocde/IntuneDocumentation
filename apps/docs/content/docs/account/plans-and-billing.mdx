The web app is free. The desktop app is a paid subscription with two plans: **Pro** for one organization and **MSP** for partners who document many customer tenants. Both plans include the same features. MSP adds more tenants and switching between them.

## Compare the plans

| --- | --- | --- |

Current prices, in EUR, are on the [pricing page](https://intunedocumentation.com/desktop#pricing), where you can also buy a plan. VAT is added at checkout where applicable.

## Money-back guarantee

Every plan comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied, email [support@ugurlabs.com](mailto:support@ugurlabs.com) within 30 days of your first payment for a full refund.

- The guarantee covers the first payment of a new subscription. It applies once per customer and does not cover renewals.
- Refunds are processed through Polar, our merchant of record, and the license key is revoked when the refund is issued.

The full conditions are in the [terms](https://intunedocumentation.com/terms#desktop-app).

## After you buy

Your license key arrives by email right after checkout. You paste it into the app during setup, or later under <Ui>License and account</Ui>. The app activates the key for the tenant you signed in to. See [Sign in and activate your license](/desktop/sign-in-and-license).

## Installations per tenant

Each tenant can have up to **5 active installations** of the app. An installation is one computer where the app is activated for that tenant.

- **Pro** covers one tenant with up to 5 installations.
- **MSP** covers 10 tenants by default, or the tenant count you purchase, with up to 5 installations per tenant.

When a tenant reaches the limit, the app shows the message "This tenant already has 5 active installations." Free an installation you no longer use, as described below.

## Share a license with colleagues

Colleagues in the same tenant can use your license without their own key. Once a license is shared with a tenant, anyone who signs in to that tenant's Intune Documentation app registration is licensed automatically. Your key stays on your machine.

- **Pro:** the license is shared with its tenant automatically.
- **MSP:** sharing is off until you turn it on per customer tenant. On the machine that holds the key, open <Ui>License and account</Ui> and turn on <Ui>Let other admins in this tenant use this license</Ui>.

Only the machine that holds the license key can change sharing. When your colleagues sign in, the wizard tells them their organization already has a license and no key is needed.

<Note>
  Each colleague's computer still counts as an installation for that tenant.
</Note>

## Deactivate a machine

Deactivate the app on a computer you no longer use, so the installation becomes available again.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open License and account

In the app, open <Ui>License and account</Ui> in the sidebar.
</Step>
<Step>
### Deactivate

Select <Ui>Deactivate this machine</Ui>. The app confirms: "This machine was deactivated. You can use the key on another machine."
</Step>
</Steps>

No longer have access to that computer? Release the installation in the customer portal instead.

<Note>
  <Ui>Clear data</Ui> under <Ui>Settings</Ui> also deactivates the license on this machine when possible. If the licensing service does not confirm the deactivation, the app tells you so and offers <Ui>Open customer portal</Ui>, where you can release the installation yourself.
</Note>

## Manage your subscription in the customer portal

Polar is our merchant of record and handles payments, taxes and invoices. In the Polar customer portal you can:

- Download invoices
- Change or cancel your subscription
- Change the tenant count of an MSP plan
- Find your license key again
- Release installations and tenants you no longer use

To open the portal, select <Ui>Manage subscription</Ui> under <Ui>License and account</Ui> in the app, or go to the [customer portal](https://polar.sh/ugurlabs/portal) and sign in with the email address you used at checkout.

### Cancel

You can cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and the app stays licensed until then. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel.

## Offline grace period

The app checks your license with our licensing service regularly while it is online. If the service cannot be reached, the app keeps working for **14 days** after the last successful check.

Collection and export only need to reach Microsoft Graph, so short outages or travel without access to our service do not interrupt your work. What a license check sends is described on [Security and privacy](/security).
