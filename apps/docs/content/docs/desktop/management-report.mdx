## A security summary management can read [#management-report]

Management does not want a long evidence report. They want to know three things: how well are we protected, where are the gaps, and is it getting better. The management report answers exactly that, on one page, in plain language.

It is a security summary in two parts, in English or German, for any [supported framework](/desktop/compliance-evidence#frameworks), such as the NIS2 Directive or ISO/IEC 27001.

- **Page 1 is for management.** It leads with one number, safeguards in place: how many of the technical safeguards behind the framework are configured in an assigned Intune or Conditional Access policy. A bar per measure shows where protection is missing (with more than eight measures, the eight weakest), followed by the five recommended next steps. It uses plain language and no control ids.
- **The following pages are for the IT reviewer.** They explain how each number is calculated and what is not counted, and list every safeguard per measure with its state and the policies behind it, including whether each policy is assigned. Every number on page 1 links to its explanation.

<Callout type="info" title="A coverage figure, not an audit result">
  Safeguards in place is a coverage figure from your Intune and Conditional Access configuration. It is not a compliance score or an audit result. Measures outside Intune, such as organisational measures, need their own assessment.
</Callout>

## The management summary in the app [#summary]

You find everything for the management report at the top of a framework in <Ui>Compliance Evidence</Ui>, under <Ui>Management summary</Ui>. It shows the same numbers as page 1 of the report, so you can check them before you export.

<Screenshot src="/images/desktop/management-report.png" alt="The NIS2 Directive management summary: Safeguards in place 66%, 40 of 60 safeguards, 1 measure with no safeguards, 3 set up but not switched on, no baseline loaded, the EN and DE switch, Export management report, Next actions, and the Baseline and Crosswalk cards" caption="The management summary for NIS2 in the fictional Contoso tenant: 40 of 60 safeguards are in place." width={2880} height={1800} />

- **Safeguards in place.** The headline percentage and the count, for example <Ui>40 of 60</Ui>, plus how many measures have at least one safeguard.
- **Measures with no safeguards.** Measures where not a single safeguard is set up and switched on.
- **Set up but not switched on.** Safeguards that are configured in a policy that is not assigned to anyone. These are often the quickest wins.
- **Change since last report.** The change in percentage points since the baseline you loaded. Shows <Ui>No baseline</Ui> until you load one.

Select a tile to filter the <Ui>Mapped controls</Ui> list below to those controls. Select it again to show all controls.

**Next actions** lists up to five recommended steps, such as configuring a missing setting, assigning an existing policy or resolving conflicting policies. Each one names the measures it affects and has an <Ui>Open in Intune</Ui> or <Ui>Open in Entra</Ui> button that opens the right place in the admin center.

If some measures are outside Intune scope, a line below the tiles says how many. Expand it to see which ones.

## Export the management report [#export]

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose a framework

Open <Ui>Compliance Evidence</Ui> and choose a framework, for example <Ui>NIS2 Directive</Ui> or <Ui>ISO/IEC 27001</Ui>. Check the platforms in scope. See [Set the scope](/desktop/compliance-evidence#scope).

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose the language

Select <Ui>EN</Ui> for English or <Ui>DE</Ui> for German.

</Step>
<Step>

### Export

Select <Ui>Export management report</Ui> and choose where to save the PDF. When it is saved, select <Ui>Open file</Ui> or <Ui>Show in folder</Ui> (<Ui>Show in Finder</Ui> on macOS).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Show progress month by month [#monthly-progress]

A single number is a snapshot. A number that moves is a story. With a baseline, the report shows how safeguards in place changed since your last report, overall in percentage points and per measure.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Save a baseline with this report

Under <Ui>Baseline</Ui>, select <Ui>Save baseline</Ui> and keep the file with your report. The file is named after the framework and the date, for example `intunedoc-baseline-nis2-2022-2555-2026-10-01.json`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Next month, collect again

Collect your tenant again so the assessment reflects the current configuration. See [Collect your configuration](/desktop/collect).

</Step>
<Step>

### Load last month's baseline

Select <Ui>Load baseline</Ui> and choose last month's file. The card shows <Ui>Compared with baseline from</Ui> and the date, and <Ui>Change since last report</Ui> shows the change, for example <Ui>+5 pts</Ui>. Select that tile to see which measures changed.

</Step>
<Step>

### Export the new report

Select <Ui>Export management report</Ui>. The report now includes the change. Save a new baseline for next month.

</Step>
</Steps>

### Baseline rules [#baseline-rules]

- **What a baseline contains.** Control identifiers, statuses, safeguard counts and the tenant ID. Never policy names or settings.
- **Same tenant, framework and scope.** A baseline only compares with the same tenant, framework and scope. A file from another tenant, for another framework or with a different platform selection is rejected with a message that says why.
- **Do not edit the file.** A baseline that was changed after it was saved is rejected.
- **Versions.** Baselines saved with version 0.2.2 need 0.2.2 or newer, so update every installation that loads them.
- **Rule updates.** If the app's mapping rules changed since the baseline was saved, the card says so, because some movement may come from mapping updates rather than your configuration.
- **Session only.** A loaded baseline is kept in memory while you are signed in. Select <Ui>Clear</Ui> to remove it. After a restart or sign out, load it again.

If a baseline is rejected, the message tells you what to do. See [Baseline file is rejected](/help/troubleshooting#baseline-file-is-rejected).

## Add CIS Controls through your own crosswalk [#cis-crosswalk]

Many organisations report against CIS Controls as well. The app ships no CIS Controls content, but you can add your own mapping, so CIS Controls safeguards appear next to your ISO/IEC 27001 and NIS2 controls.

The crosswalk works with ISO/IEC 27001 and the NIS2 Directive. On other frameworks the <Ui>Crosswalk</Ui> card only says that crosswalk ids show on ISO 27001 and NIS2.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Download the template

Under <Ui>Crosswalk</Ui>, select <Ui>Download template</Ui> and save the CSV file.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in your mapping

Fill in your organisation's mapping from your licensed CIS copy. The template has these columns:

| Column | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| <CopyValue value="iso27001_control" /> | One ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A control, such as 8.1 or A8.1. |
| <CopyValue value="nis2_measure" /> | A NIS2 Article 21(2) measure, written as 21.2.j, j or a national code such as 10A. Separate several with \| or +. |
| <CopyValue value="cis_safeguard" /> | The CIS Controls safeguard ids from your licensed copy. Separate several with + or \|. |
| <CopyValue value="notes" /> | Optional free text. |

Fill in `iso27001_control` or `nis2_measure` (or both) plus `cis_safeguard` on every row. Comma and semicolon separated files both work. Lines starting with # and blank lines are ignored.

</Step>
<Step>

### Import it

Select <Ui>Import crosswalk</Ui> and choose your file. The app confirms how many rows it imported and lists any lines it could not read, with their line numbers.

</Step>
</Steps>

After the import, mapped controls show your CIS ids as <Ui>CIS (your crosswalk)</Ui>, and the reviewer pages of the management report list them next to each measure. Select <Ui>Remove</Ui> to take the crosswalk out again.

<Note>
  Your crosswalk stays in memory for the current session and is never saved by the app, so import it again after restarting. The file can be up to 1 MB. If the import fails, see [Crosswalk import fails](/help/troubleshooting#crosswalk-import-fails).
</Note>
