## Contact support

Send a request through the [support page](https://intunedocumentation.com/support). The form asks for your <Ui>Name</Ui>, <Ui>Email address</Ui>, a <Ui>Subject</Ui> and a <Ui>Message</Ui>.

You can also email [support@ugurlabs.com](mailto:support@ugurlabs.com) directly, for example when you want to attach a file.

In the desktop app, open <Ui>Settings</Ui> and select <Ui>Get support</Ui> to go to the support page.

## What to include in your request

A good request gets a faster answer. Describe what happened and what you expected, and add:

- **Which edition** you use: the web app or the desktop app.
- **The app version and your operating system**, for the desktop app. Both are in the diagnostics file below.
- **The exact message** you see, copied as text or as a screenshot.
- **What you did just before**, for example signing in, collecting, exporting or activating a license.
- **For licensing service connection problems:** select <Ui>Details</Ui> under the message in <Ui>License and account</Ui>, then <Ui>Copy details</Ui>, and paste the text into your request.

<Callout type="warn" title="Leave out sensitive data">
  Please leave out passwords, access tokens and tenant data.
</Callout>

### Attach a diagnostics file

The desktop app can save a diagnostics file for support. It contains the app version, system details, your tenant ID, your license plan and status, collection counts and recent log lines. It contains no license key, tokens or policy contents.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Settings

In the desktop app, open <Ui>Settings</Ui>.
</Step>
<Step>
### Export diagnostics

Under <Ui>Diagnostics</Ui>, select <Ui>Export diagnostics</Ui>. The app tells you where it saved the file.
</Step>
<Step>
### Send it

Email the file to [support@ugurlabs.com](mailto:support@ugurlabs.com), or mention in your support form request that you can send it.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Billing, invoices and your subscription

Payments, invoices and taxes are handled by Polar, our merchant of record. Use the Polar customer portal to:

- Download invoices
- Change or cancel your subscription
- Change the tenant count of an MSP plan
- Release installations you no longer use

Open it from the desktop app with <Ui>Manage subscription</Ui> under <Ui>License and account</Ui>, or go to the [customer portal](https://polar.sh/ugurlabs/portal) and sign in with the email address you used at checkout. See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).

For a refund under the 30-day money-back guarantee, or for an invoice, purchase order or quote before you buy, email [support@ugurlabs.com](mailto:support@ugurlabs.com).

## Report a bug or request a feature

Use the [support page](https://intunedocumentation.com/support) for bugs and feature requests too. Tell us what you are trying to achieve, so we understand the need behind the request.
