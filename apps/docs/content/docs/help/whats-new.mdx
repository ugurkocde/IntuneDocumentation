This page lists what changed in each version of the desktop app, newest first. The app tells you when an update is available and installs it when you choose. See [Settings and updates](/desktop/settings-and-updates).

Installers for every version, including older ones, and the full release history are on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/ugurkocde/IntuneDocumentation/releases?q=desktop-v).

## Version 0.3.2 [#v0-3-2]

Released October 8, 2026.

### Improved

#### Search settings
- **Refreshing your data keeps your search.** When a new collection finishes while Search settings is open, the results update in place. Your query and filters are only cleared when you sign out or switch tenant.
- **Clear recovery when a search fails.** An error now offers Try again, or Collect when there is no collection yet.
- **Filters stay visible.** Selected family and platform filters are always shown, also while a search runs. When filters hide every match, the page says how many settings match without them and offers Clear filters.
- **Long values are readable.** Long values such as certificates or XML can be expanded with Show full value, and every value can be copied.
- **Tidier results.** Long family lists collapse behind More, the search box stays visible while you scroll, and single letters are no longer highlighted.

### Fixed
- Screen readers now announce when a search is running and read the new match count instead of the previous one.
- Result cards no longer appear as separate page regions to assistive technology.

## Version 0.3.1 [#v0-3-1]

Released October 8, 2026.

### New

#### Search settings
A new **Search settings** page in the sidebar finds any setting across all collected policies, by name, value or technical id. Type "BitLocker", "require device encryption" or a setting id such as device_vendor_msft_bitlocker_requiredeviceencryption, and the results show every policy that sets it, with the configured value, the platform and how the policy is assigned.

- Results are grouped by policy and can be filtered by configuration family and platform.
- Select a policy to open it in its section.
- Search runs on your last collection in the open app, so it also works offline.

### Improved

#### An overview that counts what you configured
The overview headline now shows **policies and profiles** only. Apps, roles and scope tags, and items Microsoft creates in every tenant are counted on their own cards:

- **Apps** shows how many apps are assigned out of all apps in the tenant.
- **Assignment and RBAC** covers roles, role assignments, scope tags, filters and reusable settings.
- **Microsoft defaults** covers built-in roles, the Default scope tag, the default enrollment configurations, tenant settings, Microsoft published remediations and connectors that are not set up.

Nothing is left out of your documentation: every item is still collected, browsable and exported as before.

#### Clearer configuration families
- Endpoint security policies and security baselines now have their own families instead of appearing inside Settings Catalog.
- Policies created from the older endpoint security templates are labelled "Endpoint security (legacy templates)" instead of "Security Baselines".
- The Apple enrollment program token is listed under Connectors.

## Version 0.3.0 [#v0-3-0]

Released October 8, 2026.

### New

#### Redesigned compliance evidence reports
Every framework report has a new design, built to hand straight to an auditor, an insurer or a customer questionnaire.

- **A new cover** shows the share of controls with technical evidence at a glance, with the document control details underneath.
- **An executive summary** with the share of controls with technical evidence, the number of safeguards in place, counts per status, coverage per control family and the key findings.
- **A results overview** lists every control with its status, how many of its capabilities have evidence, the evidence references and the page of its detail section.
- **Clearer control details.** Each capability has its own card with a table of expected and actual values, the policies behind it and whether each policy is assigned. Settings and values are shown with their names from Intune, such as "Require Device Encryption: Enabled", with the technical name underneath. Gaps and conflicting settings are highlighted, and the parts of a control that need an organisational review are listed separately.
- **A cleaner evidence register** with a short guide on how to read the report.

This applies to ISO/IEC 27001, NIS2, SOC 2, NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-171, NIST CSF 2.0, Cyber Essentials, ASD Essential Eight, BSI IT-Grundschutz (in German) and Def Stan 05-138.

#### Sample reports
Sample evidence reports for every framework, based on a fictional tenant, are now available on intunedocumentation.com, so colleagues and auditors can see what a report contains before you share your own.

### Improved
- Status is always shown with a shape and a label as well as a colour, so the reports stay readable when printed in black and white.
- Every control, status, evidence reference and count from 0.2.x is still in the report, so reports from 0.2.x and 0.3.0 can be compared directly.
- The German BSI report no longer contains English terms such as "Not found".

## Version 0.2.2 [#v0-2-2]

Released October 1, 2026.

### New

#### A security summary for management, with explanations for IT
The management report is now a security summary in two parts.

- **Page 1 is for management.** It leads with one number, **safeguards in place**: how many of the technical safeguards behind the framework are set up and switched on in Intune. A bar per measure shows where protection is missing, followed by the five recommended next steps. Plain language, no control ids.
- **The following pages are for the IT reviewer.** They explain how each number is calculated and what is not counted, and list every safeguard per measure with its state and the policies behind it, including whether each policy is assigned. This lets IT answer management's questions from the same document.
- NIS2 measures carry German titles in the German report.

#### Safeguards in place
Until now a measure counted as covered as soon as one of its safeguards was in place, so a measure with 2 of 33 safeguards looked the same as one with 2 of 3. The app and the report now show safeguards in place, overall and per measure, for example "2 of 33". Measures with at least one safeguard in place remain visible as a second figure.

### Improved
- The management panel in the app uses the same numbers and wording as the report, and each control shows its safeguards in place.
- Baselines now record safeguards in place, so the next report shows the change in percentage points and per measure. Baselines saved with 0.2.1 or earlier still load; for those, the safeguard change starts with your next saved baseline. Baselines saved with 0.2.2 need 0.2.2 or newer, so update every installation that loads them.

## Version 0.2.1 [#v0-2-1]

Released October 1, 2026.

### Fixed

- **Technical check results no longer count on/off settings that are not configured.** Compliance policies, device restriction profiles and endpoint protection profiles report every on/off setting, and one left at "Not configured" reads as off. These were counted as different values: a compliance policy that only requires the firewall, for example, added "different value" results for BitLocker, encryption and antivirus. They are now ignored. Administrative template and custom (OMA-URI) settings are unchanged, because "Disabled" there is an explicit choice.
- **No more false "switched off" findings.** An untouched Defender section in a device restriction profile was reported as switching off behavior monitoring, network inspection or virtualization-based security. It now counts as not configured. Evidence coverage is unchanged by this fix, because these defaults never counted as evidence.
- **Next actions for unassigned policies.** A setting with a non-enforcing value in a policy that is not assigned is now suggested as "Configure", not "Review policies that switch off", since it does not reach any device.

### Improved

- The technical check tiles show how many results come from assigned policies. Check results compare every policy, assigned or not, while evidence coverage only counts assigned policies.

## Version 0.2.0 [#v0-2-0]

Released October 1, 2026.

### New

#### Management report
A one-page PDF for management and other readers outside IT, in English or German. Open **Compliance Evidence**, choose a framework and select **Export management report**.

- **Evidence coverage**: the share of controls that Intune can evidence which have supporting configuration evidence. It is a coverage figure, not a compliance score or audit result.
- **Key metrics**: controls with evidence, controls without evidence, unassigned security configurations, and change since the last baseline. Each metric links to a detail page in the same PDF.
- **Next actions**: the five most useful steps, ranked so that controls without any evidence come first. Each links to the matching page in the Intune or Entra admin center.
- Measures that Intune cannot evidence, such as policies, supplier security or business continuity, are counted in one line so they can be assessed separately.

#### Progress month over month
Select **Save baseline** to store this month's result as a small file. Next month, select **Load baseline** to show the change: coverage points, newly evidenced controls and regressions. The file holds control identifiers, statuses and counts only, never policy names or settings. A baseline from another tenant, another framework or another scope is rejected.

#### NIS2
NIS2 (Directive (EU) 2022/2555, Article 21(2)) is now available as the 11th framework. Six of the ten measures are mapped to Intune evidence: incident handling, vulnerability handling, cyber hygiene, cryptography, access control and multi-factor authentication. Every measure shows the matching Danish (NIS2-loven § 6, stk. 1) and German (§ 30 Abs. 2 BSIG) reference.

#### CIS Controls through your own crosswalk
Import your organization's mapping from ISO/IEC 27001 and NIS2 to CIS Controls safeguards with **Import crosswalk**, using the CSV template from **Download template** (comma or semicolon separated). Your CIS safeguard ids then appear on the matching ISO 27001 and NIS2 controls and in the management report. The app ships no CIS content and keeps your file in memory for the current session only.

### Improved
- The Overview metric cards are clickable: configurations and sections open the family breakdown, warnings open the affected family filtered to items that could not be loaded, and permission gaps jump to the missing permissions.
- Compliance controls show their Danish and German law references as labels.
