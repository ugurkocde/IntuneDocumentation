The Intune Documentation desktop app turns your Microsoft Intune configuration into documents people can actually read. It runs on your own Windows or macOS computer, signs in with your own app registration, and reads your tenant straight from Microsoft. Your configuration never passes through our servers.

It only reads. It never changes anything in your tenant.

<Screenshot src="/images/desktop/overview.png" alt="The desktop app overview after a collection, with counts for policies and profiles, apps, assignment and RBAC, and Microsoft defaults" caption="The overview after collecting the fictional Contoso tenant." width={2880} height={1800} />

## What you can do with it

- **Document everything.** Export your policies, settings, assignments, scripts and baselines as a PDF report or a Word document you can edit.
- **Find any setting.** [Search settings](/desktop/search-settings) shows every policy that configures a setting, with its value, platform and assignment.
- **Hand evidence to your auditor.** [Compliance evidence reports](/desktop/compliance-evidence) map your configuration to frameworks such as NIS2, ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2, NIST and BSI IT-Grundschutz.
- **Report to management.** The one-page [management report](/desktop/management-report) shows how many safeguards are in place, and how that changes month by month.
- **Work across tenants.** On the MSP plan, one license covers every customer tenant. See [Work with multiple tenants](/desktop/multiple-tenants).
- **Keep data on your machine.** Collection runs locally and talks to Microsoft directly. After a license check, the app keeps working for 14 days without reaching our licensing service. See [Security and privacy](/security).

## Looking for the free web app?

Intune Documentation also has a free web app at [intunedocumentation.com](https://intunedocumentation.com). It creates the same PDF and Word documentation in your browser, with our app registration and without installing anything. Graph responses pass through our server in memory only and are never stored. See [Get started with the web app](/web/get-started), or [self-host it](/self-hosting) on your own infrastructure.

| | Desktop app | Web app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where Graph responses are processed | Only on your machine | On our server, in memory only, never stored |
| App registration you sign in with | Your own, created in your tenant | Ours, consented once in your tenant |
| Search settings across all policies | Included | Not available |
| NIS2 and the one-page management report | Included | Not available |
| Many tenants | Switch tenants in the app (MSP plan) | Sign in to each tenant separately |
| Price | Paid subscription, see [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing) | Free |
